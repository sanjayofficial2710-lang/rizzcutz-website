About Rizzcutz

15+ Years of Experience.
One Vision. Confidence.

Rizzcutz is a modern men's salon founded in 2026 by Surya and Sanjay, created with a simple vision — to help every man look sharp, feel confident and leave the salon with a style he truly enjoys.

At the heart of Rizzcutz is Surya, Owner, Founder & Master Stylist, bringing 15+ years of experience in the salon and grooming industry. His experience includes men's haircuts, modern hairstyles, beard styling, grooming, hair wash, facials, hair colouring and professional styling.

After more than 15 years in the industry, Surya wanted to create a place where his experience could be directly shared with customers. That vision became Rizzcutz in 2026.

Together, Surya and Sanjay are building Rizzcutz around experienced styling, modern trends, quality grooming and personal attention for men of all ages.

Style is personal. Confidence is universal.
